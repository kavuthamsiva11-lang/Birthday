A Few More Words

If You Ever Forget

From Your Thambi 30 • 09

My Dearest Akka,

If one day you ever forget how special you are, I hope you remember this little page.

Not because a website can explain everything, but because sometimes we don't say enough to the people who matter to us.

You became one of those people in my life who cannot simply be described as “a friend” or “someone I know.”

You are my akka. My bestie. My close person. And one of those beautiful connections that I never expected but deeply value.

Our relationship doesn't need a perfect story. It doesn't need a perfect explanation. It just needs the little moments that made it real.

The random conversations. The laughs. The teasing. The small misunderstandings. The things we shared. The moments we understood each other. And all those tiny memories that slowly became something meaningful.

On your birthday, I don't want to wish only for one happy day. I want you to have many happy days after this one.

I hope life gives you reasons to smile even when you are not expecting them. I hope you meet good people. I hope your dreams find their way to you. I hope your hard days become lighter. And I hope you always remember that you are genuinely appreciated.

No matter how much time passes, some memories remain special. Some people remain special too. You are one of them.

Happy Birthday, Akka. May this new year of your life be filled with peace, happiness, beautiful memories and countless reasons to smile.

Always your Thambi — Siva
