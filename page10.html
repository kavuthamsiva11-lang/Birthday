One Final Message

Thangameyy...

Sila relationships-ku explanation theva illa. Eppadi start aachu, eppo close aachu, eppadi ivlo important aachu-nu exact-ah solla mudiyadhu.

Nee enakku appadi oru person.

Namma blood relation illa. Aana adhu namma bond-a less special-a make pannala. Sometimes heart choose panra relationships dhaan romba beautiful-ah irukkum.

Namma conversations, random laughs, silly moments, little arguments, understanding, support... ivlo small-small things dhaan namma bond-ku meaning kuduthadhu.

“Nee en life-la oru memory mattum illa... oru beautiful part.”

Unakku life-la neraya happiness kidaikkanum. Nee dream panra things ellam gradually achieve aaganum. Unakku peace irukkanum. Unna genuinely value panra people un life-la irukkanum.

Eppovume un smile genuine-ah irukkanum. Un heart peaceful-ah irukkanum. And whatever happens, nee alone-ah feel panna koodadhu.

Nee enakku always special. Adhu oru birthday feeling mattum illa. Adhu namma bond-ku irukkura oru permanent place.

“Naan eppovume iruppen, Thangameyy.”

Idhu oru dramatic dialogue illa... idhu naan unakku kudukkura oru small promise.

Life namma rendu perai different directions-la kondu ponaalum, memories maaradhu. Namma bond-oda value maaradhu.

So whenever you look back at this page, just remember one thing... somewhere, your thambi is always wishing the best for you.

Happy Birthday once again, Akka. Stay happy. Stay peaceful. Keep smiling.
