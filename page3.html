A Letter From The Heart
03
Words I Couldn't Say Enough

For My
Dearest Akka

Some feelings are too special to fit inside a simple birthday wish. So this time, I wanted to write them down.

My Dearest Akka,
30 • 09

Sila per namma life-la vandhu poiduvaanga… sila per memories-ah mattum iruppaanga… aana romba sila per mattum dhaan namma life-oda oru part-a maariduvaanga.

Enakku nee appadi oruthavanga, Akka.

Unnai first-la just oru person-ah dhaan paathiruppen. Aana konjam konjam-ah, nee enakku oru akka, oru best friend, oru support, oru comfort person-ah maaritta.

Namma relationship-ku blood connection illa… aana heart connection irukku. Adhu dhaan enakku romba special.

Happy-ah irukkumbodhu share pannanum-nu thonra person-um nee. Kashtama irukkumbodhu pesanum-nu thonra person-um nee.

Sometimes naan sollama irundhaalum, enna purinjukkura sila people-la nee romba special.

Namma bond eppadi start aachu-nu vida, adhu enakku evlo important-ah maariduchu enbadhu dhaan mukkiyam.

Nee en life-la vandhadhu oru coincidence-ah irundhirukkalam… aana nee enakku akka-ah maarinadhu oru beautiful blessing.

Innaikku un birthday. Unakku happiness, peace, success, good health, and lots of beautiful memories kidaikkanum-nu heartfull-ah wish panren.

Life eppadi change aanaalum, time eppadi pass aanaalum, namma bond enakku eppovume special dhaan.

Always your
Thambi — Siva
