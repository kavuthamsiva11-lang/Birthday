You Are More Special Than You Know.

Ten little reasons

Why You Are Special

“Why?” nu ketta one reason-la answer panna mudiyadhu. So here are ten little reasons.

01

Your Presence

Nee irukkura feeling itself sometimes everything-a konjam better-a feel panna vaikkum.

02

Your Understanding

Ella vishayathayum explain panna vendiyadhu illa. Sometimes you simply understand.

03

Your Kindness

Small things-la irukkura kindness dhaan oru person-ai memorable-a make pannum.

04

Your Laughter

Namma random conversations-la varra sirippu definitely one of my favourite things.

05

Your Support

Big words thevai illa. Sometimes just being there itself means a lot.

06

Your Trust

Trust slowly build aagum. Build aana apram adhu romba valuable.

07

Our Memories

Small moments kooda later favourite memories-ah maaridum.

08

Our Bond

Blood relation illa-nalum, heart-la family madhiri feel aagura bond.

09

Your Place

Some people have a temporary place. Some people simply become a permanent chapter.

10

Just You

Finally, no complicated reason. You are special simply because you are you.

“Some gifts can be opened. Some memories can be saved. But some people... can only be kept in the heart.”

FOR MY DEAREST AKKA

If You Ever Forget...

Oru naal namma busy aagalam. Time pass aagalam. Life different directions-la pogalam. Aana indha page open pannumbodhu, one thing mattum remember panniko — somewhere in this world, unakku always a special place vachirukkura un thambi irukkaan.
