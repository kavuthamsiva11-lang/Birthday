A Letter Within This Page

If I Could Say One More Thing

My Dearest Akka,

Sila relationships-ku exact definition kudukka mudiyadhu. Namma relationship-um appadi dhaan. Oru particular word-la explain panna mudiyadha alavukku many little feelings irukku.

Sometimes naan yosippen, “Eppadi oru person ivlo important-ah maaritaanga?” nu. Answer simple. Big moment onnum illa. Daily life-la vandha countless little moments together dhaan.

Un presence enakku always loud-ah irukkanum-nu illa. Sometimes just knowing that you are there is enough.

Enakku happiness vandhaalum share panna thonum. Something funny nadandhaalum sollanum-nu thonum. Oru difficult day vandhaalum pesanum-nu thonum. That itself says how important you are.

Namma bond eppadi start aachu-nu vida, adhu enakku enna meaning kudukkudhu-nu dhaan important.

So on this special day, one thing mattum sollanum...

Thank you for becoming one of the most beautiful people in my life.

Always your Thambi
