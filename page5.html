Words Behind The Voice

If My Voice Could Say Everything

There are some things that don't need a big explanation. Sometimes a few honest words are enough to remind someone how special they are.

01

Thank You

En life-la vandhadhukku thank you. Enakku oru akka madhiri irundhadhukku thank you. Chinna chinna moments-ai beautiful memories-ah maathinadhukku thank you.

02

You Are Important

Unakku sometimes theriyama irukkalam. But you really matter. Un presence itself enakku oru comfort.

03

You Are Appreciated

Nee panra chinna things kooda unnoticed-ah pogala. At least naan notice pannirukken. And I appreciate them more than I usually say.

04

You Are Remembered

Oru random moment-la kooda namma pesina oru dialogue nyabagam varum. Oru place paatha oru memory varum. That's how memories work.

05

You Are Special

06

A Few More Words

Listen With Your Heart

My Dearest Akka,

Indha voice note-la naan sollra words vida, naan sollama vechurukkura feelings dhaan adhigam.

Namma bond-ku perfect definition onnum illa. Sometimes akka, sometimes best friend, sometimes support system... but overall, someone very special.

Life-la namma meet panra every person namma story-la same place edukka maataanga. Sila people short chapter. Sila people memories. Aana romba sila people mattum namma story-la permanent chapter-ah maariduvaanga.

Enakku nee appadi oru permanent chapter.

Un birthday-ku expensive gift kudukka mudiyama irukkalam. Big surprise kudukka mudiyama irukkalam. But indha website-la naan enakku mudinja alavukku oru small piece of my feelings save pannirukken.

Years later indha page open pannumbodhu, indha time-la namma share pannina memories nyabagam varanum. Adhu dhaan indha whole website-oda real purpose.

Always stay happy. Always keep smiling. Unakku deserve aagura beautiful things ellam un life-la varanum.

Your Thambi — Siva
